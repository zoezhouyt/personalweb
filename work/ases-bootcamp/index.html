Programme design · 2025

The ASES London bootcamp

A ten-week programme for around seventy fellows, and the first environment I had to design rather than attend.

Role
Co-director, ASES London
When
January 2025 – June 2026

The question

I won the ASES London competition and a funded place at the Stanford Summit, and came back to co-lead the London chapter. Having been let into a room, the question was how to build one.

ASES London is the student chapter of a Stanford entrepreneurship society. It is separate from LSESU Entrepreneurs, where I spent two years on the executive; the two come up together on a CV and are not the same organisation.

What I made

A ten-week programme for around seventy selected fellows, and around £5,000 of sponsorship to run it.

This case study is still being written. What is above is the whole of what is claimed so far; the sections still to come are the detail, not a different story.
