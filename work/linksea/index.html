Rebuilding the student side of a school platform, and testing it with the students.

Role
UI/UX designer
When
2026

The question

A school platform is used every day, by people who did not choose it and cannot route around it. The students were the group with the least say in it and the most exposure to it.

Context and constraints

A middle school in Hong Kong. The platform carries three interfaces, for students, teachers and administrators; the student one was mine. It was already in daily use, so the rebuild had to land inside something people depended on.

What I made

The student-facing interface, built in JavaScript.

What changed

I tested with the students themselves and rebuilt the parts they could not complete.

This case study is still being written. What is above is the whole of what is claimed so far; the sections still to come are the detail, not a different story.
