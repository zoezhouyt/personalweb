Cognitive Map

The same set of lines, read two ways. Flat, they are flight paths between the places you have lived. Folded, they are the cortex. Drag the slider, or press Fold.

What this is testing

Whether one visual can carry both metaphors without becoming mush in the middle. The fibres never change identity — each keeps its colour, which is its region of the brain at one end and its leg of the journey at the other. Only their positions move.

In the real site this would happen once, as the opening chapter about the four countries hands over to the rest. One transformation, then the brain stays a brain.
