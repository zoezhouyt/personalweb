About

I am reading Psychological and Behavioural Science at the London School of Economics, where my work keeps returning to one question: how accurately do learners know what they know? That interest runs from my dissertation on metacognitive calibration in AI-assisted learning, through research on curiosity and misinformation, into the products I build.

Alongside research, I have spent time where learning meets the market — designing an LLM-powered policy automation at ByteDance, underwriting insurance and healthcare deals at Fosun, and founding two early-stage ventures in education and talent. I work best on problems that need both the evidence and the product.

Research

Nov 2026 – presentLondon

Metacognitive calibration in AI-assisted learning

Independent research project, supervised by Dr Luc Schneider

Developing a dissertation on metacognitive calibration and judgement among university students and adult learners working with generative AI.

The central question is whether metacognitive prompts can improve the fit between confidence and performance, and whether that improvement transfers to independent judgement once the AI is taken away.
