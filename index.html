03Prefrontal cortex · judgement

The question I keep returning to

How accurately do learners know what they know? It sounds narrow. It is not. Calibration — the fit between confidence and actual performance — sits underneath almost every decision a person makes about when to stop studying, when to ask for help, and when to trust an answer that arrived too easily.

Generative AI has made the question urgent rather than academic. A fluent answer feels like understanding. My dissertation asks whether that feeling can be corrected, and whether the correction survives once the AI is taken away.

  • Metacognitive calibration in AI-assisted learningNov 2026 – present

    Independent research project supervised by Dr Luc Schneider. Whether metacognitive prompts can improve confidence–performance calibration, transfer, and independent judgement among university and adult learners.

  • Curiosity and misinformation detectionOct 2026 – present
