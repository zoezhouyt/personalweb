Miku

Founder · 2026

A teachable AI pupil that helps learners practise metacognitive calibration.

Read case studyExplore Miku

What I built

The learner teaches Miku, an AI pupil that starts blank and answers only from what it has been taught. Before each question, the learner predicts whether Miku will get it right. The gap between prediction and result is the calibration measure.

Where it came from

Two earlier versions, a reflection chatbot and a story-relay game, both aimed at helping children reflect on their own thinking.

Where it stands

Adult friends have used it. I’m interviewing parents before any child does, so there is no child data and no claim that it works.

How guessing and processing fluency affect susceptibility to misleading claims.

I reviewed digital-safety interventions and mapped which might suit young people in Mukuru.
