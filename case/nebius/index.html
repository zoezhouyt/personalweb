The Nebius day

A one-day build where students and engineers worked with a frontier company's newest tools, with that company's engineers in the room. Tracks were split by experience, so a first-timer was never scored against someone who had already shipped. Judging rewarded whether a team could explain why it chose a model, ahead of how finished the result looked.
