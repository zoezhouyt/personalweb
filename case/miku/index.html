Where Miku stands

Miku is a teachable agent. The learner teaches Miku, who is deliberately blank and answers only from what it has been taught, then predicts question by question what Miku will get right. The distance between prediction and result is the calibration measure, shown back as a map of what the learner actually managed to convey.

Adult friends have used it. I am interviewing parents before any child does. There is no child data yet, so there is no claim that it works.
